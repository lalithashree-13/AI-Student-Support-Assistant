1. ATTENDANCE POLICY
- Minimum Attendance: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
- Condonation: Attendance between 65% and 74% may be condoned by the Principal on valid medical grounds, provided a medical certificate is submitted within 3 days.
- Condonation Fee: A fee of Rs. 500 per subject applies for condonation.
- Lack of Attendance: Students having less than 65% attendance will not be allowed to write the end-semester exams and must repeat the semester.

2. EXAMINATION RULES
- Internal Assessment: Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT) carry 40% weightage of the total marks.
- End Semester Exam: End-semester examinations carry 60% weightage.
- Passing Criteria: A minimum of 45% marks in the end-semester examination and 50% overall (Internal + External) is required to pass a subject.
- Malpractice: Any form of cheating or copying during exams will result in severe penalties, including cancellation of all exams in that semester.

3. LEAVE & PERMISSION PROCEDURE
- Medical Leave: Must be applied through the Student Portal with a valid doctor's prescription attached.
- On-Duty (OD): OD is granted for college events, industrial visits, and sports activities with prior approval from the Head of the Department (HOD).
- Maximum OD: A student can claim a maximum of 10 ODs per semester.

4. COURSE REGISTRATION & SYLLABUS
- Re-appear / Backlog: Arrear exams can be registered along with regular semester exam forms.
- Revaluation Fee: Students can apply for revaluation within 7 days of result publication by paying Rs. 400 per paper.
- Elective Courses: Professional electives can be chosen during the course registration window before the semester begins.

5. GENERAL COLLEGE RULES
- Dress Code: Formal dress code is mandatory from Monday to Thursday. College T-shirts and jeans are permitted only on Fridays.
- ID Card: Students must wear their college ID cards at all times inside the campus.
- Cell Phones: Use of mobile phones inside classrooms and laboratories is strictly prohibited.
